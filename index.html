Book Builder

🎨 Cover Designer

Design a beautiful cover in the style of the bestselling kids activity books: your title and name, a bright theme, an ages badge and an activities badge. The same design becomes page 1 of your book and the front of the KDP wrap cover.

Cover settings are saved on this device, so your design is still here next time.

The book gets a title page, numbered activity pages (no duplicates), and a Solutions section at the back. In the print dialog choose Save as PDF and set margins to None — the page size is set automatically.

KDP Wrap Cover

Builds a print-ready front + spine + back cover sized exactly from your book: your trim size, the total page count, KDP white-paper spine width and 0.125 in bleed. It uses your Cover Designer theme, title and name; the back cover lists what is really inside your book.

Build your book first — then the wrap cover can be sized from it. Use the book's Download / Print PDF button to save the cover as a print-ready PDF.

Set your counts and click Generate Book. The full book preview appears here.
